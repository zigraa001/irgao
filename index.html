The Challenge

Why India needs air mobility

01

Congestion

  • ₹2.1 lakh crore lost every year
  • Hours a day stuck in city traffic
02

Delays

  • Ambulance response in Tier-2 cities: 14+ minutes
  • Road travel times are hard to predict at peak hours
03

Limited access

  • Many hill and remote routes have no fast road option
  • Hospitals, campuses, and industrial sites sit off the highway

Technology

What runs the platform

01

Routing and scheduling

  • Hub-and-spoke network across cities and campuses
  • Routes and departure slots planned by the system
  • Air time estimated from distance and cruise speed
02

Fleet coordination

  • Live aircraft position, status, and ETA
  • Operators and admin work off the same dispatch board
  • Reassignment when an aircraft goes out of service
03

Scale

  • One system for air taxi, air ambulance, and drone delivery
  • New cities and pads added as configuration, not rewrites
  • Built to take eVTOL aircraft as they are certified
